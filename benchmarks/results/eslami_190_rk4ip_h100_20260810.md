# Eslami 190-mode RK4IP CP profile on H100 SXM

Date: 2026-08-10

Device: NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3

Configuration:

- 190 scalar spatial modes
- 16,384 temporal samples over 20 ps
- fixed RK4IP step of 37 um
- `precision=:mixed`
- `backend=:cuda_cp_optimized`
- 5 unmeasured warm-up steps and 30 synchronized timing samples
- component timings use 20 synchronized samples

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1e-2 | 1024 | 10.5151 | 10.5234 | 10.3932 | 0.0510 | 95.10 | 2.953 |
| 1e-3 | 1280 | 12.9826 | 12.9063 | 12.5745 | 0.1822 | 77.03 | 3.501 |
| 1e-4 | 1664 | 16.3494 | 16.3381 | 16.0950 | 0.0674 | 61.16 | 4.934 |

The step cost is close to linear in CP rank over this range. At the same fixed
step size, 0.20 m requires 5,406 steps, corresponding to approximately 56.8,
70.2, and 88.4 seconds of steady-state RK4IP stepping, respectively. These
projections exclude compilation, setup, final output conversion, and I/O.

Each fixed RK4IP step evaluates the nonlinear RHS four times. The fitted CPs
have real but untied factors, so each RHS uses three forward projection GEMMs
and one backprojection GEMM. The profile therefore contains 12 projection
GEMMs and 4 backprojection GEMMs per step.

The profiler itself adds synchronization and collection overhead. The
synchronized timing distribution above, rather than profiler wall time, is
the performance result. CUPTI is used to attribute device activity only.

Raw timing data and aggregated/chronological CUPTI profiles are under
`benchmark_cache/eslami_190_cp/rk4ip_profile_h100/`. The reproducible driver
is `benchmarks/profile_eslami_190_rk4ip_cuda.jl`.
